# VOLTA Corporate — Roadmap

Corporate improves representation, trust transfer and routing. It does not accumulate product features.

## Now

Maintain the September corporate hub baseline: clear company introduction, three concrete product destinations and a separate development/exploration chapter. Preserve current product facts after releases and domain changes. Exact public treatment is documented in [CURRENT_STATE](CURRENT_STATE.md), not duplicated as another lifecycle table here.

Use `Product selected` to establish a routing baseline. Observe whether visitors understand product differences and development labels. Do not claim acquisition or comprehension gains from implementation quality alone.

Keep the static runtime, dependencies, responsive experience and metadata healthy. Complete one verified integration/deployment per coherent work package rather than consuming previews as an editing loop.

## Next

- Reconcile Corporate after material product/portfolio changes, using Strategy for classification and each product for shipped capability and operational destinations.
- Make small, evidence-led improvements if visitors choose the wrong product or cannot explain what VOLTA does.
- Introduce real case studies, customer work or testimonials only when evidence and publication permission exist.
- Adopt a centrally approved final symbol/wordmark when that controlled brand deliverable is ready.

## Later

Promote Shield, Bridge or Automate into available-product navigation only after authoritative readiness and Brand/Strategy decisions support it. A deployed prototype is insufficient.

Consider build-time portfolio synchronization only if repeated manual drift justifies it. Do not add runtime coupling, shared identity or a content backend for hypothetical future needs.

## Completed direction

The September 2026 hub implementation replaces the old concise Web 2.0 presentation, resolves Instrument Sans/green foundation drift, updates Portfolio's canonical domain, adds an honest development chapter and introduces reproducible browser verification. [CORP-DDR-001](decisions/CORP-DDR-001-corporate-hub.md) preserves the decision; GitHub/Vercel own actual execution and release status.

Previous Flow/Web 2.0 initiatives are historical. They are not instructions to reintroduce retired implementation.
