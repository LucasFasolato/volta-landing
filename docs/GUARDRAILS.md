# VOLTA Corporate — Guardrails

## Source of truth

- Corporate represents VOLTA; it does not redefine it.
- Global brand/ecosystem facts come from `volta-os`.
- Product-specific claims come from the corresponding Product OS/current implementation.
- Do not market planned/experimental capability as production functionality.

## Brand/UX

- Premium, minimal, clear and mobile-first.
- Real product storytelling over generic tech decoration.
- Outcome/value language over implementation jargon.
- Preserve strong hierarchy, generous spacing and readable contrast.
- Do not make mobile product cards/previews secondary to desktop polish.

## Conversion

- Product destinations must be correct and intentional.
- Do not add fake social proof, fake metrics or unsupported customer claims.
- CTA hierarchy should help visitors choose rather than create multiple equivalent decisions.

## Architecture

- Keep the site lightweight; do not introduce backend complexity only to centralize copy prematurely.
- If registry-driven automation is introduced later, keep graceful fallback and avoid making corporate availability depend on unrelated product runtime health.

## Shipping

Verify build/type/lint as available, desktop/mobile layout, navigation, product links, metadata/SEO and accessibility-critical interactions.
