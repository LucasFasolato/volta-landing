# CORP-DDR-002 — Corporate v1 final presentation

**Date:** 2026-09-07 UTC / 2026-09-06 Argentina  
**Authority:** founder's explicit implementation request in the Corporate conversation  
**Scope:** this site's presentation; no company repositioning or product lifecycle promotion

## Problem

Repeated visual iterations left a dense ecosystem diagram, mixed stock/illustrated imagery, redundant editorial/process chapters and too much explanation. Prior screenshot QA could succeed without proving external backgrounds had actually loaded. Local current-state documentation had drifted from the six-section runtime.

## Decision

Freeze a five-chapter baseline: company/directory → three product showcases → principles → development → closing. Retain the approved headline, Instrument Sans, canonical green, The Shift and interim logo. Use an editorial directory rather than a pseudo-dashboard. Alternate product compositions without adding interactivity that cannot be used.

Remove the stock gallery and all externally loaded photographs from Corporate. Capture the existing public product demonstrations at high resolution, preserve their content, identify them as examples, and bundle responsive WebP files with source provenance. Do not describe these images as authenticated application sessions or client results. Link to Store's existing public storefront example for an actual product destination.

Give Shield the primary future-product presentation, using its current general-purpose company AI definition. Keep En validación and non-GA MVP wording. Keep Bridge secondary with professional/project language and fair-scope framing. Keep Automate exploratory with no launch date. These layout priorities do not rewrite Strategy's VALIDATE/INCUBATE classification or formal BET policy.

Replace defensive copy with short affirmative explanations, while retaining decision-useful maturity boundaries. One strong closing replaces redundant process instructions.

## Verification and delivery

Decode all image assets before screenshots; verify their dimensions, aspect ratios, format and byte budgets. Exercise actual production HTML/JS across desktop/mobile, links, analytics, focus, text spacing, forced colors and selected automated accessibility rules. Inspect the resulting screenshots manually as well.

Iterate in an isolated branch with Vercel previews disabled **for that branch only**. GitHub source/media capture jobs do not deploy Vercel. Remove the temporary asset-writing workflow before merging. Batch implementation and docs, pass the quality gate, recheck upstream, squash merge, then verify the one production release and public URL.

## Limits and review triggers

Automated checks are not accessibility certification. Product demonstrations are not market evidence. No measured comprehension/conversion/trust improvement is claimed. Reopen this baseline for real product facts, visitor evidence or defects, not merely another visual preference cycle.
