# VOLTA Corporate — Product Role

## Role

VOLTA Corporate is the public front door of the VOLTA ecosystem. It explains the common idea behind VOLTA, presents the active products and routes visitors to the correct solution.

It is a corporate/marketing surface, **not another product line**.

## Core jobs

1. Make VOLTA understandable quickly.
2. Show that VOLTA is an ecosystem of simple, professional digital products.
3. Explain each product through the problem/outcome it addresses.
4. Direct qualified visitors to Store, Booking, Portfolio and future products.
5. Maintain coherent brand perception across the ecosystem.

## Product principles

- Simple things on the surface, complex work hidden underneath.
- Outcome before technology.
- Premium without visual noise.
- Real product previews before abstract marketing decoration.
- Mobile-first storytelling.
- No claim should outrun the product it represents.

## Non-goals

- storing independent product roadmaps;
- duplicating detailed Store/Booking/Portfolio feature documentation;
- presenting experimental ideas as generally available;
- becoming a generic agency/services website disconnected from the VOLTA product ecosystem.
