# CORP-ADR-002 — Ecosystem-first corporate positioning

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Class:** Product / brand architecture
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-25

## Context

The first landing iterations positioned VOLTA primarily as a WhatsApp/automation system. By August 2026, the verified ecosystem contained Store, Booking and Portfolio as distinct product branches, with automation retained as an incubation direction. The narrow channel-first framing no longer represented the product reality.

## Decision

VOLTA Corporate will lead with the **ecosystem**, not a single channel or implementation mechanism.

- The current approved corporate expression is **“Tu próximo paso, online.”**
- Store, Portfolio and Booking are presented through their distinct customer outcomes and product-specific previews.
- WhatsApp remains visible where it is materially part of a product (for example Store order handoff), but it does not define VOLTA Corporate or the whole brand.
- Incubating/provisional branches are omitted from general-availability storytelling until global/product truth supports publication.
- Credibility must come from real products and evidence, not invented metrics or generic SaaS claims.

## Consequences

- A future agent must not revert the main site to WhatsApp-only/chatbot positioning without an approved strategic decision.
- Adding a new public product requires checking `volta-os` lifecycle/naming plus the product's own state.
- Product showcases may look different because each domain should be explained through its real interaction model.
- The exact campaign copy can evolve, but changing the primary corporate promise/headline is a material positioning change requiring human approval.
